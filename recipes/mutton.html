Mutton Roast!

The Mutt Itself

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Stock
  • Rosemary
  • Leg of Mutton
  • Celery
  • Carrots
  • Garlic
  • Flour
  • Pepper
  • Salt
  • Onions
  • Juniper Berries

The Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 190 C / 375 F / 170 FAN / Gas 5.
  2. Peel the onion and cut into 1 cm slices, rough chop the celery and carrots and slice the garlic across the cloves.
  3. Arrange the vegetables in the base of a large roasting dish, along with the rosemary and juniper berries.
  4. The combination of chopped carrots, onion and celery is known as a 'mirepoix' in French, and it's the basis for many sauces. The fat and juices from the meat will cook the vegetables and they act as a trivet to keep the circulation of heat around the meat.
  5. Pat the mutton dry and poke the fat witha knife or a carving fork. This will help it to render down in the oven. Season with the salt and pepper. Place the leg of mutton on top of the vegetables.
  6. For a medium roast cook for about two and a half hours. If you have a meat thermometer the internal temperature should be around 65 degrees.

*Please note that this recipe is largely abridged and the rest must be felt out with your viking instinct.
